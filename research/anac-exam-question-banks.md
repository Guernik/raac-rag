# ANAC theoretical exam question banks as an eval source

Researched 2026-10-02 for #28.

## Findings

### They are public

ANAC publishes question banks for its theoretical exams on argentina.gob.ar, one page per license under [Personal aeronáutico > Exámenes](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/anac/personal-aeronautico/examenes) (18 pages: PPA, Piloto Comercial con HVI, TLA, several Instructor de Vuelo variants, Planeador, Aeroaplicador, VFR Controlado, Despachante, Controlador de Tránsito Aéreo, VANT/SVANT, among others). Publication is under Resolución ANAC 306/2014. Each page says the questions "con sus respuestas corresponden al programa de instrucción reconocida" and "formará parte de la evaluación de los exámenes de idoneidad".

The older `anac.gov.ar/anac/web/uploads/...` URLs that search engines return are blocked from the agent environment (proxy 403). The argentina.gob.ar copies (uploaded 2021/05) work.

ANAC also uses a random 100-question draw from a "Banco de Preguntas de la ANAC" in TCEXAM for the MMA (mechanic) exams, with the bank published at the links in the [Anexo de la Resolución 320/2024](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/anexo_de_resolucion_320-2024.pdf). We did not confirm whether the pilot exams draw from exactly the published PPA/VFR files today.

Third-party sites (practicapiloto.com, daypo.com, studocu) repackage the same questions. Use the ANAC originals instead.

### Terms

argentina.gob.ar [Términos y condiciones](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/terminos-y-condiciones): "El Administrador licencia todos sus contenidos bajo la licencia Creative Commons Atribución 4.0 Internacional ... excepto cuando se declare lo contrario." The exam pages and PDFs declare nothing else, so reuse is allowed with attribution (source URL per case).

### Do they cite RAAC Secciones?

Mostly no, with one useful exception.

- The question lists themselves almost never cite (PPA list: 6 mentions of "RAAC", all in question wording like "las RAAC Parte 91"). Most questions are aerodynamics, engines, meteorology, navigation and performance, which the RAAC does not cover.
- The PPA "Teoría y análisis de respuestas", Capítulo 4 "Regulaciones", explains its 27 answers with explicit RAAC Sección references, e.g. "RAAC Parte 61.115 b) 5", "RAAC Parte 91.156 b)", "RAAC Parte 91.113 (d)", "RAAC Parte 9.3 a)". These pair with Capítulo 4 of "preguntas-todos-los-capitulos" by number.

### The references and answer keys are stale

The material dates from 2013-2014. Checked against Parte 61, VI Edición Enmienda I (mayo 2026), the fixture from PR #29:

- Sección numbers moved. The bank cites 61.105 as "Conocimientos aeronáuticos" and 61.115 for private-pilot readaptation; today 61.105 is "Prueba de pericia en vuelo" and 61.115 is "Repetición del examen de vuelo después de reprobar". Private-pilot rules now sit in 61.5xx (61.510 Conocimientos aeronáuticos, 61.520 Experiencia de vuelo, 61.530 Atribuciones), alumno piloto in 61.4xx.
- Answers changed. Capítulo 4 question 8 keys "Debe informarlo dentro de los 30 días" for a change of address; current 61.160 "Cambio de domicilio" requires updating the legajo with no deadline. Question 4 (30 days without flying requires readaptation by an Instructor) has no counterpart in current Parte 61; 61.140 "Experiencia reciente" uses 90 days and three takeoffs and landings.

So an answer key or Sección reference from these files cannot be trusted as an expected (Parte, Sección) or reference Answer for the RAAC vigente.

## Recommendation

Import them, but only as question candidates through the review flow of #11, never as accepted eval cases.

1. **Scope.** Take only regulation questions: PPA Capítulo 4 (27 questions with 2013 Sección hints) and the questions in the PPA and VFR Controlado lists that ask about RAAC rules (pick by keyword plus a Haiku 4.5 classification pass; expect a few dozen). Skip aerodynamics, engines, meteorology and navigation for the main set.
2. **What to keep.** Keep the question wording, which is realistic Spanish exam phrasing, and the options as context. Store the 2013 citation and keyed answer as a hint only, never as `expected_secciones` or `reference_answer`.
3. **Mapping to the RAAC vigente.** For each candidate, the review flow shows the question, the 2013 hint, and the current Sección the local pipeline retrieves. The owner accepts the mapping, fixes it, or rejects the case (for example when the rule no longer exists). The reference Answer is written from the current Sección text.
4. **Provenance.** Mark imported cases with a source such as `anac-exam-bank`, the PDF URL and question number, and the CC BY 4.0 attribution, so they stay distinguishable from generated and hand-written cases (#11 already requires that distinction).
5. **Optional out-of-scope cases.** A handful of non-regulation questions (e.g. "¿Qué indica el número de octanos de un combustible?") can become out-of-scope refusal cases, after the owner confirms no Parte covers them.
6. **Parser notes.** The TCPDF lists use a fixed layout: question number plus `S 1`, then options numbered 1-3 with `*` on the correct one, with a repeated page header and a `página N / M` footer. The chapter files use `N.- ` numbering under `CAPÍTULO N: ...` headings. Questions reference figures in a separate "Anexo Figuras" PDF; drop questions that need a figure.

Expected yield is modest, roughly 30-80 reviewed cases concentrated in Partes 61 and 91. That is useful for the Parte routing work in #7 and the coverage set in #25. It does not replace generated cases for other Partes.
